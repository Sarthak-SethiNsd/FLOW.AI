
=== adho-mukha-svanasana ===
Step 1: Start by getting down onto your hands and knees. Place your palms flat on the floor directly beneath your shoulders. Spread your fingers wide apart on the mat and point your middle fingers straight forward. Place your knees directly underneath your hip joints, with your knees and feet spaced several inches apart. Keep your back completely flat and straight from the bottom of your neck to the bottom of your back. Look straight down at the floor between your hands.
Step 2: Bend your toes under so they are resting flat on the floor. Keep your fingers spread wide and press the entire surface of your palms down into the mat. Press your thumbs and index fingers firmly against the floor. Keep your elbows straight but do not lock the joints. Keep your head looking straight down.
Step 3: Press your hands into the floor and lift both knees up in the air. Keep your knees slightly bent at first. Push your hips straight up and back toward the ceiling. Keep your arms completely straight, pushing the floor away from you. Let your head hang down between your upper arms, and look backward at your legs or feet.
Step 4: Push your hips up and back, straightening your legs. Your body should form an upside-down V shape. Press your heels toward the floor. It is acceptable if your heels do not touch the ground, but keep pushing them downward. Push your chest backward toward your thighs. Ensure your back remains completely straight from your hands all the way to your hips. If your back rounds, bend your knees slightly to keep a flat back.
Step 5: Hold this upside-down V shape. Keep your fingers spread wide, palms flat, and arms straight. Push your weight backward into your legs. Keep your head positioned between your upper arms. Press your heels firmly down toward the floor and keep the back of your legs straight. Breathe steadily, keeping your back entirely flat.

=== balasana ===
Step 1: Let us begin Balasana, or Child's Pose. Start by kneeling on your mat with your knees about fist-width apart. Lower your bottom down to sit directly on your heels. Place both hands flat on top of your thighs, with your palms facing down and fingers pointing toward your knees. Sit up straight and tall.
Step 2: Slowly breathe in through your nose. Raise both arms straight up overhead until your hands are above your head. Keep your arms shoulder-width apart with your palms facing each other and fingers pointing straight up. Keep your bottom resting on your heels.
Step 3: Breathe out slowly and bend your entire upper body forward from your hips. Keep your back straight as you lower your upper body toward your thighs. Keep your arms extended straight in front of you. Ensure your bottom remains pressed against your heels.
Step 4: Walk your fingers forward along the floor until your arms are completely straight. Lower your chest down to rest on your thighs. Gently place your forehead flat on the mat. If your forehead does not reach, simply let your head hang down.
Step 5: Stay in this position and breathe naturally. Keep your arms extended, your forehead down, and your bottom on your heels. With each breath out, let your chest sink closer to your thighs. Relax the muscles in your neck and shoulders.
Step 6: When you are ready to finish, place both palms flat on the mat directly under your shoulders. Breathe in and push against the floor with your hands. Slowly raise your upper body until you are sitting upright on your heels again. Well done.

=== bhujangasana ===
Step 1: Let us begin Bhujangasana, or Cobra Pose. Lie completely flat on your stomach on the mat. Extend both legs straight back with your feet together. Point your toes backward so the tops of your feet touch the floor. Rest your forehead directly on the mat.
Step 2: Bring both hands up and place your palms flat on the mat directly under your shoulders. Point your fingers straight forward. Bend your elbows and pull them in tight against the sides of your body. Press the tops of your feet firmly down into the mat.
Step 3: Breathe in slowly and begin to lift your head off the mat. Lift your forehead, then your nose, then your chin. Use the muscles in your back to lift your chest off the floor. Keep your arms bent and your elbows hugged against your ribs.
Step 4: Press your palms into the mat to lift your chest a bit higher. Keep your hips and the front of your hip area pressed firmly against the floor. Pull your shoulders back and down away from your ears. Look straight ahead with your neck long.
Step 5: Hold this position and breathe steadily. Keep your elbows bent and close to your sides. Keep your shoulders pulled down and your chest pushed forward. Ensure your legs are straight and the tops of your feet remain on the floor.
Step 6: Breathe out slowly and gently lower your body back down. Lower your chest, then your chin, and finally place your forehead back on the mat. Relax your arms down by your sides. Well done. You have completed Cobra Pose.

=== cat-cow-flow ===
Step 1: Get down onto your hands and knees. Place both palms flat on the floor directly under your shoulders. Point your fingers straight forward. Place your knees directly under your hips, spaced a few inches apart. Rest the tops of your feet flat against the floor. Keep your back completely flat and straight from your neck to your hips. Look straight down at the floor between your hands.
Step 2: Breathe out and push your hands into the floor to round your entire back upward toward the ceiling. Pull your stomach inward. Drop your head downward and tuck your chin against your chest. Look backward between your legs. Keep your arms straight and your palms flat on the floor. Keep your knees on the ground.
Step 3: Hold this rounded position. Keep your chin tucked securely against your chest. Keep your back curved upward as high as it will go. Press both palms and the tops of both feet firmly down against the floor. Do not bend your elbows.
Step 4: Breathe in and drop your stomach downward toward the floor, creating a downward curve in your lower back. Lift your chest and head upward, looking straight ahead or slightly up at the ceiling. Keep your arms perfectly straight and your palms flat. Push your shoulders backward, away from your ears. Keep your knees and the tops of your feet on the floor.
Step 5: Breathe out and return your back to a completely flat and straight position. Stop rounding or arching your back. Look straight down at the floor between your hands again. Keep your arms straight and your palms flat. Well done.

=== surya-namaskar ===
Step 1: Welcome to Surya Namaskar. Stand at the front of your mat. Place both feet together, with your big toes and heels touching each other. Keep your legs straight. Bring your palms together in the center of your chest, with your fingers pointing straight up. Drop your shoulders down. Keep your head straight and look forward.
Step 2: Breathe in. Keep your feet together and legs straight. Raise both arms straight up above your head, shoulder-width apart, with your palms facing each other and fingers pointing up. Bend your upper back backwards slightly, pushing your hips forward. Tilt your head back to look up at your hands.
Step 3: Breathe out. Keep your feet together and legs straight. Bend forward at your hips. Lower your arms and upper body down. Place both palms flat on the floor, directly beside the outside edges of your feet, fingers pointing forward. Let your head hang down toward your knees.
Step 4: Breathe in. Keep your hands flat on the floor. Step your right foot far back. Rest your right knee on the mat and untuck your toes so the top of your right foot is flat on the floor. Keep your left foot flat on the floor between your hands, with your left knee bent at a right angle, like the corner of a square directly over your left ankle. Lift your chest and head to look straight forward.
Step 5: Hold your breath. Press your hands into the floor. Step your left foot back to place it next to your right foot. Keep your arms straight with your shoulders directly over your wrists. Straighten both legs so your body forms a straight line from the top of your head to your heels, like a push-up position. Look down at the floor.
Step 6: Breathe out. Lower both knees down to rest on the mat. Bend your elbows straight backward, keeping your arms pressed against your ribs. Lower your chest and your chin to rest on the mat between your hands. Keep your hips lifted slightly off the floor. Your hands, knees, chest, chin, and toes should be touching the mat.
Step 7: Breathe in. Slide your body forward and lower your hips to the mat. Untuck your toes so the tops of your feet are flat on the floor. Press your hands into the mat to lift your head and chest up. Keep your elbows slightly bent and pressed against your ribs. Look straight forward.
Step 8: Breathe out. Tuck your toes under. Push your hands into the floor and lift your hips up and back. Straighten your arms and legs to form an upside-down V shape. Press your heels down toward the floor. Keep your head between your upper arms and look back toward your feet.
Step 9: Breathe in. Step your right foot forward and place it flat on the mat exactly between your hands. Lower your left knee onto the mat and untuck your left toes so the top of the foot is flat. Your right knee should be bent at a right angle, like the corner of a square directly over your right ankle. Lift your chest and look straight forward.
Step 10: Breathe out. Step your left foot forward and place it next to your right foot. Keep both feet together. Bend forward at your hips, keeping your hands on the floor beside your feet. Breathe in, stand all the way up, keeping your legs straight. Reach both arms straight up and slightly back, looking up. Breathe out, lower your arms to your sides. Well done.

=== tadasana ===
Step 1: Let us begin Tadasana, or Mountain Pose. Stand up straight on your mat. Place both your feet together, touching each other side by side. Let your arms hang naturally by your sides, with your palms facing inward toward your thighs and your fingers pointing down to the floor. Look straight ahead at a fixed point in front of you to keep your balance.
Step 2: Press the base of your big toes, the base of your little toes, and the left and right sides of your heels firmly into the mat. Keep your toes pointing straight forward. Gently squeeze the muscles in the front of your thighs. As you do this, you will feel your kneecaps lift slightly upward. This action protects your knees and tightens your legs.
Step 3: Tuck the bottom of your back slightly downward and inward. Lift your chest upward and open it wide. Roll your shoulders backward and pull them down away from your ears. Keep your chin level with the floor, not tilting up or down. Keep your palms facing inward.
Step 4: Take a slow, deep breath in through your nose. Rise up onto your toes, lifting your heels off the mat. Lift the top of your head upward toward the ceiling to lengthen your back. Keep your shoulders down and your chest open. Balance your weight evenly on the front of both feet.
Step 5: Hold this raised position and breathe steadily. Keep your heels lifted off the floor and your toes gripping the mat. Keep your leg muscles tight and your lower belly pulled in. Keep your eyes focused straight ahead to keep your balance. Notice the effort it takes to stay perfectly still.
Step 6: Slowly breathe out and lower your heels back down flat onto the mat. Release the tension in your leg and belly muscles. Let your arms hang loosely and stand in a relaxed position. Well done. You have completed Tadasana.

=== trikonasana ===
Step 1: Welcome to Triangle Pose. Stand with your body facing the side of your mat. Step your feet about three to four feet apart. Keep both legs straight. Raise both arms straight out to the sides level with your shoulders, level with the floor. Face your palms down toward the floor, with your fingers pointing straight out.
Step 2: Turn your right foot out a right angle, like the corner of a square so your toes point to the front edge of the mat. Turn your left foot slightly inward. Keep both legs completely straight. Keep your upper body and hips facing the side of the mat. Keep your arms stretched out to the sides level with your shoulders, palms facing down.
Step 3: Breathe out. Keep your legs straight and your feet in position. Reach your right arm straight forward over your right leg. Bend sideways at your waist to the right. Keep your upper body facing the side, without turning your chest toward the floor.
Step 4: Lower your right hand down and place it on the front of your lower leg, ankle, or the floor. Raise your left arm straight up toward the ceiling. Your arms should form a straight up and down line. Turn your head to look up at your left hand. Keep both legs straight, and keep your chest facing the side. Breathe steadily.
Step 5: Breathe in. Keep your legs straight. Lift your upper body back up to a standing position. Turn your right foot inward to point to the side again. Lower both arms down to your sides. Step your feet back together. Well done.

=== utkatasana ===
Step 1: Start by standing straight on your mat. Place both feet together so your big toes touch, or place them about a fist's width apart. Let your arms hang straight down by your sides with your palms facing your legs. Keep your back completely straight, press the bottoms of your feet firmly into the floor, and look straight ahead. Take a slow, deep breath in through your nose.
Step 2: As you breathe in, raise both arms straight up above your head. Place your upper arms directly beside your ears. Keep your elbows completely straight. Turn your hands so that your palms are facing each other and your fingers are pointing straight up toward the ceiling. If this hurts your shoulders, hold your arms straight out in front of your chest instead, level with the floor. Keep your shoulders pressed downward, away from your ears.
Step 3: Breathe out and bend both knees. Lower your hips backward and down, exactly as if you are sitting down on a chair. Keep your knees pointing straight ahead in the same direction as your toes; do not let your knees collapse inward. Shift your body weight back onto your heels. If you look down, you should be able to clearly see all of your toes in front of your kneecaps.
Step 4: Continue to lower your hips until your thighs are as close to level with the floor as possible. Keep your chest lifted upward and forward. Keep your arms raised straight up beside your ears, or straight in front of you. Keep a completely straight back. Your upper body will naturally lean forward at an angle, but keep your back flat, not rounded.
Step 5: Hold this position and breathe steadily. Keep the bottoms of your feet flat on the floor, your knees bent, and your arms held straight. Make sure your palms are still facing each other. With every breath out, try to lower your hips just one inch further down. Keep looking straight ahead. Your thigh muscles will feel warm and tired, which means you are building strength. Keep your chest up and your back flat.
Step 6: Breathe in, press your feet firmly into the floor, and straighten your legs completely to return to a standing position. As you breathe out, lower both arms straight down to rest beside your legs. Keep your the way you are standing tall and straight. Well done.

=== virabhadrasana-ii ===
Step 1: Welcome to Warrior Two. Stand tall at the center of your mat. Place both feet together, with your toes pointing forward. Keep your arms resting straight down at your sides. Look straight ahead.
Step 2: Step your feet wide apart, about four feet. Keep both legs straight. Place both hands on your hips. Keep your upper body and hips facing the side of your mat. Look straight ahead.
Step 3: Turn your right foot out a right angle, like the corner of a square so your toes point to the front of the mat. Turn your left foot slightly inward. Keep both legs straight. Make sure your hips and chest are still facing the side of the mat, not turned toward your right foot.
Step 4: Breathe in. Keep your legs as they are. Stretch out both arms straight out to the sides level with your shoulders, level with the floor. Face your palms down toward the floor. Point your fingers straight out. Keep your upper body straight and centered.
Step 5: Breathe out. Bend your right knee until your right thigh is level with the floor. Your right knee should be bent at a right angle, like the corner of a square, directly above your right ankle. Keep your left leg completely straight, with your left foot pressed flat into the floor. Keep your upper body straight up, with your arms still stretched out to the sides.
Step 6: Keep your legs and upper body exactly as they are. Turn your head to the right and look straight out over the fingers of your right hand. Keep your arms straight and level with the floor, palms facing down. Breathe steadily.
Step 7: Hold the pose. Keep your right knee bent at a right angle, like the corner of a square directly over the ankle. Keep your left leg straight. Keep your upper body perfectly upright. Keep your arms stretched out straight out to the sides level with your shoulders. Look out over your right hand. Breathe.
Step 8: Breathe in. Straighten your right leg. Lower both arms down to your sides. Turn your right foot inward so your toes point to the side of the mat. Step your feet back together. Well done.

=== vrikshasana ===
Step 1: Let us begin Vrikshasana, or Tree Pose. Stand up straight with your feet together and your toes pointing forward. Let your arms hang at your sides with your palms facing your legs. Look straight ahead at a fixed spot on the wall to help you balance.
Step 2: Shift your body weight entirely onto your left foot. Press the bottom of your left foot firmly into the mat. Keep your left leg straight and strong, but do not lock your knee perfectly stiff. Keep your toes pointing forward.
Step 3: Bend your right knee outward to the right side. Lift your right foot and place the flat bottom of it against the inside of your left leg. Place it either on your lower left the back of your lower leg or upper left thigh. Do not place your foot directly on the side of your knee joint.
Step 4: Once your foot is secure, bring both hands in front of your chest. Press your palms flat together with your fingers pointing straight up. Point your elbows out to the sides. Press your right foot into your left leg, and push your left leg back against your foot to stay steady.
Step 5: Hold this position and keep your eyes focused on your spot. Keep your right knee pointing out to the side. Keep your palms pressed together at chest level. Breathe slowly and steadily while maintaining your balance on one leg.
Step 6: Slowly lower your right foot back down to the mat next to your left foot. Lower your arms back down to your sides. Take a breath standing on both feet. Well done. You can repeat this on the other side.
